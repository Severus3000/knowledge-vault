# Intermediate Epidemiology

A graduate-level course covering disease measurement, causal inference frameworks, and major epidemiologic study designs.

---

## 1. Measures of Disease Frequency & Association

Quantifying disease occurrence and comparing groups.

- [[master-courses/epidemiology/measures-of-disease/intro-and-disease-frequency/article.md|Introduction & Disease Frequency]] — Open vs. closed cohorts, prevalence (counts/proportions/odds), incidence (risk/rate), Kaplan-Meier, person-time, age/period/cohort effects
- [[master-courses/epidemiology/measures-of-disease/measures-of-association/article.md|Measures of Association]] — 2x2 tables, risk/rate/odds contrasts (RD, RR, OR), when OR approximates RR, confidence intervals, p-values, risk communication

---

## 2. Causal Inference

Frameworks for establishing causation from epidemiologic evidence.

- [[master-courses/epidemiology/causal-inference/causal-frameworks-and-dags/article.md|Causal Frameworks & DAGs]] — Bradford-Hill criteria, Rothman causal pies, directed acyclic graphs, confounding/mediation/effect modification, counterfactual framework, exchangeability

---

## 3. Study Designs

Major analytic study designs in epidemiology.

- [[master-courses/epidemiology/study-designs/randomized-controlled-trials/article.md|Randomized Controlled Trials]] — RCT taxonomy, randomization, blinding, ITT vs. compliance-corrected analysis, causal identification, WHI estrogen trial example
- [[master-courses/epidemiology/study-designs/cohort-studies/article.md|Cohort Studies]] — Prospective/retrospective/mixed designs, target population, sampling, exposure measurement, exchangeability without randomization
